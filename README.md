Mealzy - A modern food delivery app concept for discovering, customizing, ordering, and tracking your favorite meals.

🍔 Mealzy – App Features

Mealzy is a food delivery mobile app UI designed to make food ordering simple and convenient.
<br>
📱 Main Features<br>
🚀 Splash & Onboarding – Introduces the Mealzy app.<br>
🔐 Login & Sign Up – Users can create and access their accounts.<br>
🏠 Home Screen – Browse available food and categories.<br>
🔍 Search – Find food items easily.<br>
🍔 Food Details – View food images, prices, ratings, and details.<br>
🌶️ Food Customization – Choose spice level, size, sides, and drinks.<br>
🛒 Cart & Order Summary – Review selected items and total price.<br>
💳 Payment – Select a payment method and complete the order.<br>
✅ Order Confirmation – Shows successful order information.<br>
❤️ Favorites – Save favorite food items.<br>
📦 My Orders – View previous and current orders.<br>
👤 Profile – Manage personal information and settings.<br>
💬 Customer Support – Chat with support when needed.<br>
🎨 UI Design<br>
Clean and simple interface
Easy navigation<br>
Food-focused design<br>
Clear buttons and icons<br>
Simple ordering process<br>
Mobile-friendly layout<br>
🔄 User Flow<br>

Login → Home → Select Food → Customize → Cart → Payment → Order Confirmation
